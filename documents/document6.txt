Energy-Efficient Smart Buildings

Energy-efficient smart buildings use technology to reduce electricity consumption while maintaining comfort and safety. Sensors can detect occupancy, temperature, daylight, and equipment activity. Smart lighting systems can automatically adjust brightness or switch lights off when rooms are empty. Automated heating, ventilation, and cooling systems can respond to changing indoor conditions. Energy meters can provide detailed information about electricity usage and help building managers identify waste. Renewable sources such as rooftop solar panels can further reduce dependence on conventional electricity. Data dashboards can show consumption patterns and support better decisions.
