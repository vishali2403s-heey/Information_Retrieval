Intelligent Traffic Signals

Intelligent traffic signals help cities manage road movement more efficiently. Traditional signals usually follow fixed timing schedules, while intelligent systems can adjust signal duration according to current traffic conditions. Cameras, road sensors, and connected devices can detect vehicle density, waiting times, and congestion. Traffic information can be analyzed to coordinate signals across nearby intersections. During emergencies, signals may provide priority to ambulances and other emergency vehicles. Better signal coordination can reduce unnecessary stops, fuel consumption, travel time, and vehicle emissions.
