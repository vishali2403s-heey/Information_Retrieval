Urban Air Quality Monitoring

Urban air quality monitoring helps cities understand pollution levels and protect public health. Monitoring stations can measure pollutants such as particulate matter, nitrogen dioxide, carbon monoxide, and ozone. Small sensors can be installed near roads, schools, industrial areas, and residential neighborhoods. Collected information can be displayed through digital dashboards or mobile applications. Authorities can identify pollution hotspots, compare air quality across locations, and observe changes over time. Public alerts can warn residents when pollution becomes unhealthy. The information can also support decisions about traffic control, industrial activities, and environmental policies.
