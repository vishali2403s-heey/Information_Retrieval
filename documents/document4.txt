Smart Parking Systems

Smart parking systems help drivers find available parking spaces in busy urban areas. Parking sensors can detect whether individual spaces are occupied or empty and transmit availability information to a central platform. Drivers can view nearby spaces through mobile applications or electronic signs before reaching their destination. Some systems allow users to reserve spaces and complete digital payments. Real-time parking information can reduce the time spent searching for a space, which may lower traffic congestion and fuel consumption. Parking operators can also use collected data to understand demand and improve space management. Smart parking technology makes urban travel more convenient
