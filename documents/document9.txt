Smart Street Lighting

Smart street lighting systems improve public lighting while reducing energy consumption. Traditional streetlights often operate at the same brightness throughout the night, even when roads are empty. Smart lights can use motion sensors, timers, and environmental information to adjust brightness according to activity and conditions. LED lamps consume less electricity and generally require less maintenance than older lighting technologies. Connected lights can report faults automatically, helping maintenance teams identify problems quickly. Central software can monitor thousands of lights and analyze energy usage.
