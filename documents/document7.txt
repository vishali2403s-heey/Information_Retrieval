Public Bicycle Sharing Systems

Public bicycle sharing systems provide convenient and environmentally friendly transportation for short urban journeys. Users can locate bicycles through mobile applications and unlock them using digital codes or membership accounts. Bicycle stations are placed near residential areas, offices, schools, railway stations, and public spaces. GPS and usage data can help operators understand travel patterns and redistribute bicycles between busy and quiet locations. Bicycle sharing can reduce dependence on private vehicles, traffic congestion, fuel consumption, and transport emissions.
