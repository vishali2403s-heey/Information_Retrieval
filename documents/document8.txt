Urban Noise Pollution Monitoring

Urban noise pollution monitoring helps cities identify areas where excessive sound affects residents and public spaces. Noise sensors can measure sound levels in decibels near roads, airports, construction sites, factories, schools, and residential neighborhoods. Monitoring platforms can store measurements and create maps showing noisy locations and time patterns. Authorities can use this information to investigate complaints, regulate construction activities, and improve traffic management. Long-term noise data can help planners design quieter public areas and protect sensitive locations such as hospitals and schools. Public dashboards can increase awareness of noise pollution.
