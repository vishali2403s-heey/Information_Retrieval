Smart Waste Management

Smart waste management uses technology to improve how cities collect, separate, recycle, and process waste. Smart bins can contain sensors that measure the amount of garbage and send information to collection centers. This helps workers identify full bins and plan efficient collection routes. Data about waste levels can reduce unnecessary vehicle trips, fuel consumption, and operating costs. Separate containers can support recycling of paper, plastic, glass, and metal. Mobile applications can help residents report overflowing bins or illegal dumping.
